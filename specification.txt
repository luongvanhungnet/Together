TOGETHER GAME SPECIFICATION
Paradox Lab | 18 November 2026
https://paradox-lab-six-puzzles.luongvanhungnet.chatgpt.site/together.html

GAME RULES
Slide all foods in one direction. Pieces nearest the leading edge move first.
Each piece slides until blocked by a wall, another piece, or the board edge.
Animal tiles do not stop or lock pieces. All foods must reach their matching
animals simultaneously. Blocked inputs do not count. There are no lives or timers.

PIECES AND TARGETS
Orange carrot → rabbit. Yellow banana → monkey. White/light gray bone → dog.
Blue fish → cat. Two-piece levels use carrot and banana. Three-piece levels add
fish. Four-piece levels add bone. The legend reflects the active pairs.
Full-color Twemoji illustrations and labels distinguish foods and animals.
Food tiles are solid, animal targets have dashed borders, and matched foods
become smaller badges so their animals stay visible. Artwork is CC BY 4.0.

CONTROLS AND FEEDBACK
Swipe, tap on-screen arrows, or use arrow keys or WASD. Hold an on-screen arrow
to preview the destinations, then release without moving. The preview is free
and does not count as help. Sliding is animated with reduced-motion support.
Undo (also Z) restores previous positions, including after a win. Redo restores
undone moves and survives reload. A different move clears the redo branch.
Restart resets the current attempt and its hint use. The board shows move count,
blocked inputs, matched targets, hint feedback, and completion feedback.

CAMPAIGN AND PROGRESSION
23 freely selectable levels. Level 1 is a two-move tutorial. Levels 2–20 are
the main progression, with up to 50 moves required. Levels 21–23 use four foods
and have minimum solutions of 24, 28, and 41 moves. A win offers the next level.
The Next Challenge button recommends the first unsolved level and resumes it
if started. After all levels are solved, it recommends missing hint-free
medals, then missing three-star scores.

STARS, MEDALS, AND HINTS
Par is the shortest solution. Three stars require par. Two stars require more
than par but at most par + max(2, ceil(par × 20%)). Any slower finish earns one.
Hints do not affect stars. Personal records track best moves, best hint-free
moves, finishes, and hint-free finishes. Campaign totals show levels solved,
stars, and hint-free medals. The level selector displays stars and medals.
Gentle Hint highlights a useful food and its target. Reveal Next Move gives an
exact direction. Both count as help. When the solver detects a dead end, it
suggests Undo or Restart. Undo does not erase help already used. Win an
unassisted attempt to earn a hint-free medal.

DAILY CHALLENGES
The date selects a shared board from 366 puzzles and changes at midnight
Asia/Bangkok time. Dated links reopen the same board after rollover. Daily
best scores are tracked by date. Players can copy a spoiler-free result with
date, stars, moves, hint use, and link, with a manual clipboard fallback.
Current and longest streaks track consecutive same-day solves. Yesterday's
streak remains active until today's opportunity ends. The weekly panel shows
Monday through today. Solve any three distinct daily boards from that week to
meet the goal. Missed puzzles can count toward the goal but not the streak.

CHALLENGE A FRIEND
After a daily win, copy a link containing the exact date, move count, and hint
status. It contains no solution. The recipient's attempt is saved separately
from their regular daily board. The sender's score stays hidden in the UI
until the recipient wins. Compare moves, stars, and hint status after winning.
Fewer moves wins the comparison, and equal moves tie. Rematch restarts the
same board. The recipient can also send a new link with their own result.
Scores are self-reported in the link. Sharing does not grant access. The
deployed site is owner-only, so friends need access before a link will open.

SAVING AND RETENTION MEASUREMENT
Browser storage saves current board positions, move and redo history, hint use,
scores, records, and last mode. Standard daily resume selects today's board.
Dated links select their board. Saves are local to the browser, and the game
reports unavailable storage. Challenge opens, finishes, and later-day returns
after finishing within seven days of first open are stored in the browser.
There is no aggregate retention report or cross-device tracking.
